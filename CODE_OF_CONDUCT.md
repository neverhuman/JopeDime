# Code of Conduct

JopeDime is maintained by a small team and, once public, welcomes contributors from anywhere.
Everyone who participates — in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and the learning-loop
runbooks — agrees to the following.

## Our standard

- Be direct and be kind. Review the work, not the person. Evidence (a failing test, a receipt, a
  benchmark row) settles technical disagreements; volume does not.
- Assume competence and good faith. Ask before assuming a mistake; explain before rejecting.
- Respect people's time: small, reviewable pull requests; reproducible reports; no drive-by
  demands for unpaid work.
- No harassment, discrimination, doxxing, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks, in
  any project space or when representing the project elsewhere.
- Security and licensing findings are reported privately first (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)),
  never used as leverage.

## Scope

All repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, comment threads), the project's chat
channels, and any event or forum where someone is acting on behalf of JopeDime.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers listed in [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) (a private
email to the repository owner is always acceptable). Reports are handled confidentially. Responses
range from a private note, to a public correction, to temporary or permanent removal from project
spaces, proportionate to the behaviour and its repetition. Maintainers who violate this code are
held to the same standard by the other maintainers.

## Attribution

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org>),
shortened to what a small project can actually enforce.
