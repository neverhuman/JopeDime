## Evidence vocabulary

**Implemented** means source exists. **Qualified** means a named execution was measured. **Pending** means acceptance remains. **Unknown** is not zero. Historical results do not establish current capability. [§](sections/status.md#reading-this-manual-audience-evidence-levels-and-figures) gives the full evidence ladder.
