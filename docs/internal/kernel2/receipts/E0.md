# Track E0 — strict profile child admission

This is the first prerequisite of B05 under the owner decision of 2026-10-02.
`kernel::search::admit_child(text, profile, input_width)` checks parse/print exact
codec equality, static scope, profile membership and budgets, then requires a
variable leaf in every expression. It returns the original typed child or a
structured kernel error before fitting or scoring. Legacy readers, printers,
wrappers and RNG streams retain their existing paths.

The two kernel2 revision-1 declarations admit pipelines with explicit terminal
profile heads. Function, binary, variable and stage membership also require
the manifest's search admission; declaring an unpromoted element in a caller's
profile cannot qualify it. Scope uses the post-transform lower width; profile
columns are additionally bounded below 8. Expression caps are 15 nodes, depth 4,
and at most three carriers. Negation permits negative constants only. Birth
parameter distributions are not legal post-edit parameter ranges, and finite
expression constants may remain continuous after birth.

Acceptance command:

```sh
cargo test --locked -p feat-math --test kernel2_search
```

Eight nonignored tests cover exact typed fixed points, the three documented
lossy printer adversaries, signed zero, scope, profile membership, variable
leaves and expression/stage caps. One million deterministic random admission
fixtures span widths 1, 3, 8, 20 and 600, every declared stage name, the weighted
head entries, and the expression function pool. They do not qualify a shipping
generator, parameter sampling distributions, runtime fitting or emitter closure.

Independent review of the first candidate reproduced a caller-profile bypass:
removing the carrier declaration and listing `map` as an ordinary stage skipped
expression validation. A related terminal-head reclassification also bypassed it.
The correction identifies actual `map`/`derive` roles independently of the lists,
requires their declaration and pool, and requires an actual terminal learner.
The added regression fails on the original candidate and covers omitted/altered
carrier declarations, stage/head reclassification, unpromoted functions,
constant-only expressions, illegal negation, wide columns and depth overflow.
