# Red-team audit and hardening plan — 8 September 2026

The historical recommendation to retain the production seeder remains supported. The experimental tooling has several concrete isolation, recovery, and verification defects. Passing the original checks was insufficient to establish operational robustness.

This audit changed only this document and artifacts under `runs/red-team-20260908/`. It ran disposable local fixtures and read remote results. It launched no new real GP evaluations or GPU jobs, changed no production services, and did not open the original final-test artifacts. Findings below describe defects and limitations; their remediation has not yet been implemented.

**Evidence independently checked**

- The 17 existing comparison, binary workflow, and scheduler Python tests passed.
- All 5,419 saved candidate result identities were checked; all 5,285 successful results matched their bound metric files and target-scale conversion. No mismatches were found.
- All 1,024 quality scores shared by current-first64 and larger-pool rankings were identical, despite different scoring batches.
- Read-only inspection on the remote node reconciled all 48 downstream best scores against 4,780 observations. No exact program repeated within a search. None of the initial candidates was changed by vocabulary projection; the 417 projections were evolved candidates.
- Production was still running `/home/ubuntu/jope-dime/parallel/bin/jope-parallel-cpu-v44`, with 13,128 completed GP views at the remote inspection timestamp. This is a point-in-time observation, not a guarantee about subsequent activity.
- Six adversarial fixtures exposed weaknesses that the passing suite missed. See [probe results](../runs/red-team-20260908/probe-results.json), [reproduction script](../runs/red-team-20260908/probes.py), and [remote observation audit](../runs/red-team-20260908/remote-downstream-audit.json). The probe results bind the reviewed source hashes.

**Findings, in remediation order**

P0 means fix before another unattended experiment. P1 means fix before using the infrastructure to support a promotion decision. P2 means address before broadening the claims or scaling the work.

**What the seed result establishes**

The saved larger-pool best8 ratios remain 1.09079 against the pure first64 control and 1.08612 against generic seeds. The rejection is strongly influenced by `sidc-sunspots`, whose family ratios are approximately 1.90 and 2.01. Removing that family as a diagnostic changes the aggregate to 1.00735 against first64 and 0.99477 against generic. Even this post-hoc subset fails the original requirement of at least 2% improvement against both controls. The family must remain in the primary result.

The result supports retaining the existing production seeder and investigating the combined selector. It does not justify a broad claim that larger candidate pools are intrinsically worse. The downstream result should be treated as descriptive qualification under interrupted budgets. Fourteen of sixteen larger-pool winners were retained teachers, which limits sensitivity to proposal quality.

**Hardening plan**

1. **Contain writes and own worker lifetimes. Estimated effort: 0.5–1 engineering day. Addresses R1–R2.**

   Introduce one small shared experiment-runner boundary for the Python entrypoints. Require an experiment manifest to reside in a dedicated output directory; reject the workspace root, shared `runs` directory, production campaign paths, and protected stores. Validate derived output paths and prevent symlink traversal. Use descriptor-relative, no-follow writes or an equivalent private output mount to close the check/write race. Retain the native search guard as a second check.

   Launch experimental controllers and descendants in an owned cgroup or equivalent supervised scope. Read-only mount production data/model inputs and permit writes only in the experiment and its private temporary directory. Make the resource lease owned by the supervisor until the worker group is empty. Parent-death signals are useful defense in depth but do not by themselves cover grandchildren or a supervisor crash. Move proposal-rank cleanup inside the lease context. Add one controller lock to proposal and binary runners, acquired before loading resumable state.

   **Acceptance:** R1's symlink fixture is rejected before any external write. Kill one rank, the controller, and the supervisor at launch and during work; all descendants exit, and neither lease becomes usable while workers remain. Repeat with SIGTERM, SIGKILL, a worker exception, and partial launch failure. Exercise a mandatory GPU handoff and verify zero experimental overlap with the production block. Shared CPU affinity remains at most 16 total, rather than 16 per workflow.

2. **Make results and restart state self-validating. Estimated effort: 0.5–1 day. Depends on step 1; addresses R3–R4 and part of R7–R8.**

   Define a typed evaluation identity containing canonical exact program bytes, source and membership hashes, preprocessing hash, evaluator and relevant library hashes, task, scoring implementation/units, RNG seed, timeout, memory, threads, and numerical environment. Bind this policy in the frozen manifest. Current constants must either match and validate that policy or be removed in favor of it; no silently ignored manifest limits or gates.

   Validate every loaded result against the expected key, allowed status, finite nonnegative values, metric hash, and derived RMSE conversion. Recompute downstream winners and aggregate summaries from immutable observations. Reject foreign keys and inconsistent cached values. Keep failure observations bound to their timeout/resource policy; a timeout under one policy is not an exact terminal answer under another.

   Use stable attempt IDs and a durable state transition record for admitted, started, metric-committed, cancelled, failed, and complete work. Recover only a torn final journal suffix; reject corruption in complete earlier records. Reconcile a durable result missing its ledger entry without refitting or double-counting it. Persist the next GP proposal counter alongside the committed observation so RNG state cannot lag. Harvest exact atomic native candidate metrics left before a journal commit.

   **Acceptance:** corrupt each identity/metric field independently and require rejection. Inject termination before and after every publication boundary. Completed durable results are never refitted; uncertain interrupted work is explicitly recorded and retryable. Attempt counts, logical coverage, successful fits, failures, and elapsed costs reconcile after every restart. State explicitly that a crash before any durable result may require repeating computation; do not promise physically exactly-once execution across an unobservable failure.

3. **Strengthen binary publication and reproduce the full cohort. Estimated effort: 0.5–1 day plus bounded GPU windows. Depends on steps 1–2; addresses R5–R6 and R11.**

   Replace runtime assertions with explicit validation errors. Require the exact admitted job-key set to equal each component's complete key set and the context index's key set. Reject duplicates, omissions, unexpected extras, wrong counts, incorrect dimensions/byte lengths, NaN/Inf values, wrong task/family assignments, and broken component→context→projection links. Verify class coverage from the reconstructed memberships, duplicate/entity group disjointness, within-repetition fold coverage, and unique membership hashes. Missing or mismatched initial overlap must fail verification.

   Build a minimal, explicit child environment. Reject semantic overrides such as `JAIN_HYPERION_NO_NORMALIZE` and `JAIN_HYPERION_FULL_CANVAS`; bind effective environment, Python/native dependencies, driver/library versions, GPU model, precision, seeds, and dispatch policy. A completed stage must carry and validate an inventory of its outputs. Write preparation/stage artifacts into staging directories, fsync them, and atomically publish completion. Directory existence alone must not imply successful preparation.

   Rerun all 80 cuts independently in serial dispatch from fresh output directories, including target, foundation, Hyperion, and assembled contexts. Compare all bytes and semantic receipts. Vary legal scheduling and GPU rank assignment separately; define exact reproducibility for the frozen hardware/software configuration and explicit numerical tolerances for portability checks. Keep the original failed batched replay and both original indexes unchanged.

   **Acceptance:** missing a single context or raw component fails publication; the empty-index probe fails. Normal and `python -O` launches enforce identical checks. Semantic environment changes fail before resource admission. All 80 same-configuration replay pairs are exact, or the claim is narrowed and the discrepancy remains a failed gate. Restart after stage deletion/corruption either deterministically repairs an unpublished stage or rejects a changed immutable artifact. No cut CSV copies appear. Report four source families, actual admitted/completed counts, and remaining grouping limitations separately.

4. **Freeze an unambiguous experimental protocol. Estimated effort: 0.5 day. Can use read-only analysis during earlier steps; addresses R8–R11.**

   Preserve v1 as historical qualification. Create a new experiment version that freezes the driver/report implementation, complete policy, exact checkpoint/corpus/vocabulary, alias map, all preprocessing/encoder inputs, code/dependency bindings, and selection lineage before inference. Audit all checkpoint-selection candidates and decisions before the freeze, including rejected checkpoints; verify the assumption that their panels match rather than relying on that comment in code. Exclude their cuts and campaign anchors explicitly.

   Specify behavior for zero RMSE, one/all candidate failures, ties, incomplete prefixes, and missing families. Report finite quality and failure coverage as separate quantities; never silently remove an all-failed arm from an aggregate comparison. Freeze whether secondary outcomes are all-success conditional estimates or budgeted best-success estimates. Retain both when useful, but label their denominators. Make the frozen manifest the report's actual policy source.

   Name the existing control `pure_first64`. If assessing the running production system, add a separately labeled, exact v44 end-to-end control using its real filtering and shared tails, while keeping it outside the pure-prediction claim. Test the first64 control against a golden reference with identical checkpoint, context, seed, device, vocabulary, and fallback behavior. Separate candidate origin, neural selection, GP inheritance, and GP evolution in provenance.

   For diagnosis, predeclare a small set of informative ablations: all valid neural draws without the bank, bank-only proposals, grammar-only proposals, and the combined pool. Retain checkpoint/context/random controls only where they answer a distinct question. Expand full-forward/decoder-free parity checks to bank heads, longest supported programs, multiple batch sizes, pool permutations, and GPU ranks. Verify that teacher and held-out-label mutations cannot alter pure outputs using both dependency inspection and sentinel fixtures.

   **Acceptance:** a machine validates the complete protocol before any outcome access; modifying it requires a new version. Golden current-control parity passes, including underfilled batches. Every selected program traces to exact vocabulary support and its complete origins. No parameter snapping inherits an old score. No result is described as independent-family testing when it reuses development families.

5. **Repeat downstream qualification with a fair budget. Estimated effort: 0.5–1 day plus admitted compute windows. Depends on steps 1–4; addresses R7–R10.**

   First recompute diagnostics from existing results without new fits: family and cut ratios, leave-one-family-out sensitivity, failure rates by arm/prefix/head/origin, origin composition, and prediction-versus-measured-quality ordering. Do not discard Sunspots or tune a new selector and assess it on these same outcomes as fresh evidence.

   Run matched view/seed blocks with balanced arm order and multiple predeclared seeds. Separate the evaluation-count endpoint from the wall-time endpoint. For the latter, run each matched block in a sufficient admitted window or charge measured active interrupted work identically; do not use unequal blanket resume penalties. If a safe production handoff interrupts a block, mark that block incomplete until its predeclared fairness rule is satisfied. Continue giving production priority.

   Keep retained/exploration tails identical, count how many pure candidates actually execute, and report improvement over the inherited baseline separately. Report uncertainty clustered by source family and seed variability. Add genuinely new source families before treating repeated cuts as additional independent evidence. The four binary training families provide encoding coverage, not binary held-out model validation; add separate source families for that later question. Keep Sonar qualification-only until entity/acquisition grouping is resolved or its limitation is explicitly admitted for a bounded claim.

   **Acceptance:** matched seeds and effective budgets reconcile across arms; restart preserves committed observations and next proposal counters. Every full-family result includes every predeclared family. The promotion decision consumes only the frozen rule, with uncertainty and failure coverage visible. The present selector stays undeployed unless a new, properly qualified result passes the gate.

6. **Publish a verifiable release and dashboard. Estimated effort: 0.5 day after the preceding gates; addresses R12.**

   Replace hard-coded experiment paths with an explicit active-experiment registry pointing to immutable manifests. Show production-selected versus experiment-frozen checkpoints separately. Display admitted contexts, verified components, published contexts, exact replay coverage, evaluated teachers, and attributed seed wins as separate counts. GPU reasons should reconcile scheduler lease ownership, live worker process groups, admission requests, and timestamp freshness; show unknown state when evidence conflicts.

   Provide a manifest-driven verifier and a documented restore command that includes or locates every remote dependency, including downstream observations. Freeze the compiled executable, Rust/Python sources, dependency lockfiles, vendored code, and container/environment identity together. Create an isolated tracked change set for this work without absorbing unrelated workspace edits. Preserve the original experiment and reports; write corrected interpretation and new results as versioned artifacts.

   **Acceptance:** reproduce the readout from a clean checkout plus the declared artifact bundle. A missing remote dependency produces an exact missing-input error. Corrupt a receipt or make it stale and verify that the TUI no longer reports that item as verified or active. Repeat five-view/resize/reconnect checks. Run an unattended canary spanning a full production cycle, a mandatory GPU handoff, and a forced controller failure; observe no out-of-scope writes, orphan workers, duplicate committed fits, resource-budget violation, or unexplained status claims.

**Release gate and effort**

Allow roughly 3–5 engineering days, with end-to-end and statistical qualification taking additional elapsed time when GPU windows or new source families are unavailable. The dependency order matters more than elapsed time: operational containment and result integrity must pass before running more qualification work. Use local fixtures and small CPU tests first; real comparisons and binary encoders must continue sharing one scheduler allocation.

Release requires R1–R8 fixed and their adversarial checks passing, an independently recomputed report, a fully verified binary inventory with accurately scoped replay claims, and a successful production-coexistence canary. Resolve or explicitly carry the R9–R12 scope limitations in the versioned protocol and dashboard. Operational readiness and demonstrated model improvement are separate gates: hardening the runner alone does not justify deploying the larger-pool selector.

Strict exact-result caching should follow the validated evaluation identity from step 2. Then optimize repeated fits and overlap predictor export with corpus preparation while preserving immutable inputs and measured counters. Doing either before the cache and lifecycle contracts are correct would amplify the defects found here.
