\section{The Tabular Black Hole: All-Data Random-Split Training}
\label{sec:blackhole}

Standard foundation-model pretraining treats benchmark corpora as off-limits forever, while operational enterprise deployments face the exact opposite requirement: when a company owns a fleet of recurring tables, every historical row should strengthen the model's weights. We formalize this consolidation setting as the \emph{tabular black-hole} training regime, governed by three hard invariants:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Sealed holdouts remain physically outside the input closure.} Sealed evaluation cohorts (\code{CONFIRM}, \code{HOLDOUT}, \code{SEALED} public tasks, \code{FMGAP B/C}, and the $32$-table bank-disjoint validation panel \code{30ccaa2a}) are excluded by content hash before any bank assembly.
  \item \textbf{All training episodes use random non-official partitions.} When a table of $N$ rows enters an absorption pool (whether in the generalist foundation bank or in a per-family/per-table dossier), each training episode draws a fresh pseudorandom support/query split $(\pi_{\mathrm{sup}}, \pi_{\mathrm{q}})$ with random feature-subspace views, random row subsampling, and target-monotone coordinate jitter. Official benchmark train/test partition indices are never passed to the trainer.
  \item \textbf{Scoring remains strictly inductive on official splits.} At evaluation time on an official split $(I_{\mathrm{train}}, I_{\mathrm{test}})$, all normalization statistics, target charts, out-of-fold stack weights, and context tokens are computed exclusively from $I_{\mathrm{train}}$; $y_{I_{\mathrm{test}}}$ is mechanically blocked by a Python runtime \code{sys.addaudithook} guard during prediction generation.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Generalist all-data corpus scale.}
Our live 5-host training fleet (\code{xbabe1}--\code{xbabe4} plus dedicated CPU harvester) trains five concurrent $M$ ($23.7\mathrm{M}$) and $L$ ($104.4\mathrm{M}$) generalist checkpoints on \code{Bank r5} (digest \code{41c8aafb}, comprising \BHRFiveTables{} real tables across \BHRFiveFamilies{} families). As of 16 November 2026, the fleet has processed \textbf{\BHTotalDatasetsFed{} cumulative dataset episodes} (\textbf{\BHAllDataDatasetsFed{} episodes} under the post-ruling all-data random-split policy) at a sustained throughput of $233$--$350$ datasets/s across five nodes, preparing the expanded \BHRFSixTargetTables{}-table \code{Bank r6} corpus paired with $59{,}849+$ multi-split zero-loser teacher realizations.
