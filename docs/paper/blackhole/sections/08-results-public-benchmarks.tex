\paragraph{Published R2 reference field and Wave~A external baseline calibration (\cref{tab:wave-a-kumo}).}
To separate upstream published results from our local sandboxed harness before scoring \EZ{}, \code{LOOK\_LEDGER} line~60 scores all $44$ public regression tasks strictly from official \code{data.tabarena.ai} R2 artifacts through 2026-11-12 (\code{f9af8e90}; \emph{NOT a Wave~A read: Ours and local external runs not included}, so no number from line~60 is compared against \EZ{}) \est{}. Across all $36$ \code{BeyondArena} public regression tasks (\code{all\_36\_full\_coverage}, $40$ methods), \code{TA-KUMO-TABULAR} ($1666.3$ Elo $[1523.6, 1871.2]$) leads \code{TA-KUMO-TABULAR-MEDIUM} ($1591.3$), \code{TA-KUMO-TABULAR-SMALL} ($1544.7$), and \code{TA-TABPFN-3.5} ($1519.0$ $[1403.4, 1670.8]$); on the $28$ \code{BeyondArena} tasks where all four foundation-model families published rows (\code{common\_28}, $42$ methods), \code{TA-KUMO-TABULAR} ($1740.4$ $[1574.9, 2012.8]$) and \code{TabArena}'s retrained \code{TA-LIMIX-2-1016} ($1729.8$ $[1545.5, 2051.7]$) lead \code{TA-TABPFN-3.5} ($1589.1$) and \code{TA-TABFM} ($1483.8$), while on the $8$-task \code{TabArena} slice ($75$ methods, wide intervals) \code{TA-LIMIX-2-1016} ($2684.9$ $[2353.0, 5800.8]$) and \code{TA-KUMO-TABULAR} ($2684.9$ $[2460.6, 3622.2]$) tie at $\#1$ ahead of \code{TA-TABPFN-3.5} ($2347.7$ $[2165.1, 3011.8]$) and \code{TA-TABFM} ($2255.7$ $[2070.5, 2919.1]$; in the earlier 2026-09-22 snapshot \code{b2728e4c}, withdrawn \code{TA-LIMIX-2 (default)}, release checkpoint \code{ed01f7a0}, HF \code{de07b679}, which upstream found contaminated with benchmark data, \code{autogluon/tabarena\#648}, makers' audit 2026-11-11, and \code{TabArena} dropped 2026-11-16 for \code{LimiX-2-1016}, had scored $2453.9$). Independently, our locked offline Wave~A run (\code{R\_K} \code{f63ff1ce}, $88/88$ completed tasks) calibrates our local harness ($1702.7$ and $1624.1$ Elo on the $29$-task pre-refresh slice; $5$--$0$--$2$ on the $7$ \code{sub8192} tasks) \est{}. Once local \code{LimiX-2} (\code{R\_L}, lines 56--57), \code{TabFM-1.0 Single} (\code{R\_F}, line 54), \code{TabPFN-3.5} ($44/44$, line 61), and our unabsorbed/absorbed runs complete under \code{v3.6} (\code{Rule R-c}), all \EZ{} comparisons are fitted strictly per regime \pre{}.
