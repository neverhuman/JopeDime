\section{Training experience}
\label{app:corpus}

\paragraph{What counts as experience} A unit of experience is a tuple
$(s,a,y,\mathrm{receipt})$: a state, a complete committed candidate, its verified outcome and a
receipt binding the source commit, evaluator identity, input hashes and resource use
(\cref{sec:training}). Candidates without a receipt, outcomes computed on confirmation families and
outcomes whose evaluator identity differs from the declared comparison identity are excluded. Every
exclusion is counted.

\paragraph{Learning procedures (existing)} The program's corpus holds 14,987 verified runs over 3,003
dataset versions, 7,120 contexts and 1,068 parent lineages, drawn from a real tier of 3,859 datasets
\est{} (\cref{app:history}). Outer test folds and sealed meta-test families are outside the input
closure of every training worker. Dataset labels are kept intact; derived targets are separate
versions in the same family.

\paragraph{Next-experiment decisions (proposed)} Optimization histories from public tuning
benchmarks \citep{pineda2021hpob}, from the standard black-box suite \citep{hansen2021coco} under
declared instance transformations, and from published reaction-optimization data
\citep{shields2021reaction,ahneman2018cn}. Histories are produced by every A10 tool at declared
budgets, so the frozen histories of track CH are not authored by EurekaZero.

\paragraph{CAD parts (proposed)} Specification families generated procedurally from bracket, mount
and clamp templates with randomized envelopes, interfaces, loads and processes, plus families derived
from public engineering parts with simulation \citep{whalen2021simjeb}. Every part in the experience
store was built, meshed and analysed by the declared verifier \citep{geuzaine2009gmsh}, and failed
attempts are retained as negative experience.

\paragraph{Teacher searches} Each game's teacher is a verified search procedure run at declared
budgets on training families only. Its outputs enter the store as experience; its internals are
protected (\cref{app:disclosure}). An arm whose teacher searches are not seeded by the model (the
search-only teacher, \cref{fig:ablation}) bounds what seeding contributes.
