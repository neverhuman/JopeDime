\section{Statistical analysis plan}
\label{app:statistics}

\paragraph{Unit and estimand} The inferential unit is the dependency component; the estimand is the
family-weighted mean paired difference $\Delta$ (\cref{app:formal}). Problems within a family are
averaged first, so a family with many problems does not dominate.

\paragraph{Intervals} 95\% intervals are bias-corrected and accelerated \citep{efron1987bca}, from
10,000 paired cluster-bootstrap resamples of components, with both arms resampled together.

\paragraph{Primary tests and multiplicity} One primary test per game: superiority of A1 over the
locked comparator with margin 0.02 utility units, one-sided, Holm-adjusted across the three games at a
familywise 5\% level \citep{holm1979}. ``All three games'' requires all three adjusted tests.
Non-inferiority claims (A1n, the shared model) use the same margin and are secondary. No secondary
result is reported as confirming unless its interval clears the margin.

\paragraph{Failures and missing data} Failures score $-1$ and stay in every denominator. A problem on
which the verifier fails for every arm is excluded and counted; a problem missing for one arm is a
failure of that arm.

\paragraph{Comparison identity} Every comparison binds the task, dataset hash, cut, evaluator
version, solution-language version and protocol. Results with different identities are never pooled,
and a change of evaluator identity starts a new comparison.

\paragraph{Power} Sample sizes come from simulation using development-only variance and failure
rates. A game whose power at the margin falls below 0.8 is declared a pilot before unblinding and
reported without a confirmatory claim.

\paragraph{Search-equivalent budget} $B_{\mathrm{eq}}$ is computed on the measured grid
$\{1,2,4,\ldots,256\}$ in each bootstrap replicate; its interval is the 2.5th and 97.5th
percentile grid points. When the grid maximum does not reach the first proposal, the result is a bound.

\paragraph{Subgroups} Subgroups (rows, features and noise for A; dimension and noise for B; load cases,
contact and process for C) are predeclared, reported with intervals and never used for a confirmatory
claim.

\paragraph{Registration} The analysis plan, rosters, comparators, margins and this appendix are
frozen and hashed before confirmation runs; deviations are listed with their reasons.
