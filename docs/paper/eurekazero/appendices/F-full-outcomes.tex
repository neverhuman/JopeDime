\section{Full outcomes}
\label{app:outcomes}

\Cref{tab:outcomes} reports the failure-inclusive utility of every arm of \cref{tab:arms} on the
confirmation families of each game. Track-S arms spend declared budgets and are shown for reference;
they are never compared with first proposals without their budgets. A11 lists the strongest model
from the amortized literature in each game: TabPFN for learning procedures, whose output is a
prediction rather than a procedure \citep{hollmann2025tabpfn}; the strongest of the pretrained
optimizers for next experiments; and the strongest text-to-CAD model at one attempt for parts.
