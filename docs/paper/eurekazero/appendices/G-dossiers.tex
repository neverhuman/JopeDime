\section{Discovery dossiers}
\label{app:dossiers}

\paragraph{Protocol} A dossier is opened for an N4 proposal whose new component can be isolated as a
named part of the artifact. Opportunities are examined in order of the committed artifact's hash,
lowest first, until the dossier budget is spent; no proposal is chosen for looking interesting. Each
dossier records, before any intervention: the proposed mechanism in one paragraph; the component that
implements it; the nearest known alternative and why it is nearest; and the reserved problems on which
it will be tested (60 per dossier, from families not used in training or confirmation). The
interventions are removal at matched capacity and replacement by the nearest known alternative. N5
requires both effects to have interval bounds above zero and a prior-work review, by a reviewer not
involved in the proposal, that finds no equivalent.

\paragraph{Outcome} \syn[EZ-G15]{\EZdossA} learning-procedure and \syn[EZ-G15]{\EZdossC} CAD dossiers
were opened. \syn[EZ-G15]{\EZnFiveA} and \syn[EZ-G15]{\EZnFiveC} reached N5; the others failed at
removal (the component was not needed), at replacement (a known alternative did as well) or at
prior-work review. All are listed in \cref{tab:dossiers}.
