\section{Compute ledger}
\label{app:compute}

\paragraph{Metering} Teacher searches, training and proposals are metered separately. CPU time is the
sum over processes of user and system time; GPU time is device-occupied wall time. A measurement
nested in another (a verifier call inside a search, say) is recorded once, at the innermost level.
Frontier-model calls are metered by the provider's usage report.

\paragraph{Rebound} Cheaper proposals can raise total compute if they are used far more often, as
cheaper coal raised coal consumption \citep{jevons1865}. Break-even counts are therefore statements
about cost per problem at matched quality, not about total spending.
