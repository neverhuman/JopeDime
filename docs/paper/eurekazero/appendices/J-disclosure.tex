\section{Disclosure boundary}
\label{app:disclosure}

Two components are protected: the internal construction of the solution-embedding stage, and the
internals of the teacher searches that generate training experience. Everything needed to check the
paper's claims is disclosed. \Cref{tab:disclosure} draws the line.

\begin{table}[h]
\caption{What is released, what is available for audit, and what is protected.}
\label{tab:disclosure}
\centering\tablesf
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.2\linewidth}LL@{}}
\toprule
Item & Status & Why it suffices \\
\midrule
Protocol, counters, supervisor & released & the Zero contract is checked by code, not by our report \\
Benchmarks, rosters, verifiers & released, with the sealing salt after unblinding & every first proposal can be re-verified \\
Every committed artifact & released, with receipts & first proposals can be inspected and re-run without EurekaZero \\
Adapters, decoders, solution languages & released & what the model can and cannot express is public \\
Proposer checkpoint & released for evaluation, excluding third-party weights & every first-proposal result can be reproduced \\
Solution-embedding contract & released and tested (\cref{app:model}) & the stage's inputs, outputs and guarantees are verifiable \\
Solution-embedding internals & protected & not needed to reproduce or audit any result \\
Training experience & released for the public families, as $(s,a,y,\text{receipt})$ & training can be reproduced from the experience \\
Teacher-search internals & protected; budgets, interfaces and outputs released & what the teacher produced, and at what cost, is public \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\paragraph{What the boundary costs} A reader can reproduce every evaluation and, from the released
experience, the training of the released proposer. A reader cannot regenerate the experience with our
teacher. We state this as a limitation of reproducibility at the training-data level, the same
limitation that applies to any model trained on data the reader cannot recreate. Reviewers may audit
the protected components under a confidentiality agreement; the audit checks the four guarantees of
\cref{app:model} and that no protected component reads the verifier or the confirmation families.
