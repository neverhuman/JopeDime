\section{Gap ledger: what must replace each synthetic result}
\label{app:gaps}

Every purple number in this draft links to one entry below. An entry names the result it stands
for, the work package that must produce the measurement, and the acceptance test the measurement
must pass before it may replace the synthetic value. When an entry is closed, its number is replaced
by a receipt-bound measurement, its \code{\textbackslash syn} wrapper is removed, and the entry is
deleted; the paper may be submitted only when this appendix is empty.
