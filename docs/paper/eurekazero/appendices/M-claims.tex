\section{Claims ledger}
\label{app:claims}

Every claim the paper makes, the minimum evidence it needs, its present status and the gaps that
block it. \emph{Protocol} claims are true by construction and checked by code; \emph{synthetic north
star} claims are what the experiments must establish; a \emph{do not assert} claim must not be made
however the experiments come out.
