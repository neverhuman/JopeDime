\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Three games}

The three games were chosen so that the move, the verifier and the output role all change
(\cref{tab:domains}).

\paragraph{A: learning procedures (C0, artifact)} The state is a training table---at most 4,096 rows
and 256 features in the information-matched packet---with a declared objective and fit/predict
budget. The move is a complete, standalone fit/predict procedure. The verifier fits it on the
training rows and scores it on held-out rows over three split seeds and three folds. Objectives cover
squared and absolute error, the 0.9 quantile and exposure-weighted Poisson deviance for regression,
and log loss for binary and multiclass classification; regression with many objectives counts as
breadth within one game, not as several games.

\paragraph{B: next-experiment decisions (CH and SEQ, action)} The state is a search space and a
history of evaluated points with outcomes, feasibility, cost and remaining horizon. In \textbf{B1}
(CH) histories are built by a predeclared behaviour policy that is independent of every compared
method, each method emits exactly one point, and the verifier evaluates it. In \textbf{B2} (SEQ)
every method starts from the same initial observations and chooses its own 50 points. Families are
held-out structural function classes from the COCO/BBOB suite \citep{hansen2021coco}, held-out real
tuning tasks from HPO-B \citep{pineda2021hpob}, and reaction-condition tables from high-throughput
experimentation \citep{ahneman2018cn,shields2021reaction}, whose discrete action sets we label as such.

\paragraph{C: engineering specifications to CAD (C0, artifact)} The state is a structured
specification of a single-piece bracket, mount or clamp: interfaces, keep-out regions, load cases,
material and process. The move is an editable construction program in a bounded CAD language
\citep{cadquery2024}, exported to STEP. The verifier builds the solid, checks geometry and
specification compliance, meshes it \citep{geuzaine2009gmsh} and runs a converged linear-elastic
finite-element analysis; a predeclared subset is re-evaluated by an independently configured solver,
and a prospective subset is fabricated and load-tested. Public load cases from a jet-engine bracket
dataset anchor the generated specifications to a known engineering problem \citep{whalen2021simjeb}.

\subsection{Splits and contamination}

Splits are made by dependency component before rows, folds, transformations or parameter variants
exist: original data source for learning tasks, generating mechanism or underlying real objective for
optimization, and source design and topology lineage for CAD. Every family carries its exposure
history, and families the JopeDime program has already used for training or development are never
relabelled as confirmation (\cref{app:history}). The protocol has four levels (\cref{tab:splits}). The
locked confirmation set, its dependency graph, the archive of complete training artifacts and every
preprocessing rule are hashed and registered before any confirmation outcome is opened; a renamed file
does not make a new family.

\subsection{Controls}

Every way the result could be explained without learned proposal competence has its own control
(\cref{tab:arms}). The primary comparator in each game is the strongest \emph{non-search} control on
development data, named before confirmation; for learning procedures it is a gradient-boosted tree
selector over the complete-program archive, the control that the program's own studies found hardest
to beat \citep{jopedime2026h27}. Some baselines have a different output object---TabPFN predicts rather
than writing a procedure \citep{hollmann2025tabpfn,qu2025tabicl}---and are reported as performance
references, not forced into the primary.

\subsection{Estimands and statistics}

For each game $d$ the primary estimand is the family-weighted paired difference in failure-inclusive
utility against the locked comparator,
\begin{equation}
\Delta_d=\mathbb E_{F\sim p_d}\bigl[\,\overline U_F(\text{EurekaZero})-\overline U_F(\text{comparator})\,\bigr],
\label{eq:delta}
\end{equation}
averaging folds, seeds and repeated contexts within the dependency unit first. Intervals are 95\%
paired cluster bootstraps (BCa, 10,000 resamples, fixed seed) \citep{efron1987bca}; the three primary
claims are Holm-adjusted at a familywise 5\% level \citep{holm1979}, and a benefit ``in all three
games'' requires all three. The superiority margin is 0.02 utility units. B1 and B2 are different
estimands and are never pooled. The descriptive \emph{search-equivalent budget}
\begin{equation}
B_{\mathrm{eq}}=\min\bigl\{B:\ \mu_{\mathrm{search}}(B)\ \ge\ \mu_{\mathrm{EurekaZero}}(1)\bigr\}
\label{eq:beq}
\end{equation}
is estimated jointly on the measured grid; if no measured budget reaches the proposer we report a
bound, never an extrapolation. Sample sizes come from development-only variance and failure prevalence
(\cref{app:statistics}); a game that cannot reach adequate power is declared a pilot in advance.

\subsection{Reproducibility and measurement}

Every result row binds family, component, method, checkpoint, state hash, candidate hash, seeds,
counters, raw metrics, failure class, cost and evaluator identity. Frontier-model usage is metered by
the provider; wall time and summed CPU or GPU time are reported separately, and nested measurements are
never added. \Cref{app:repro} states which of five reproduction tiers each result reaches.
