# Editorial decisions for the InnovationZero publication

This build turns five supplied research specifications into a complete manuscript,
not another specification. It preserves a concise main argument and moves exact
source material and technical detail into separate volumes. No model training,
predictive campaign, provider-token measurement or claimed Move42 discovery was
performed to prepare it.

## Source review

The supplied specifications are planning evidence, not independent scientific
results. Their source appendices were checked against pinned repository objects.
The most recent final specification and proposal supplied the principal protocol;
the critical/comparative reviews supplied adversarial controls. The older paper
specification supplied narrative ideas whose empirical claims were rechecked.

## Decisions adopted

- **One story:** can experimental history improve the first proposed algorithm?
  The paper is explicitly a protocol and evidence audit. Publication typography
  does not certify that its prospective result has happened.
- **One proposal is not one neural pass:** one committed reusable learning
  procedure, no candidate execution before commitment, no feedback repair or
  resampling. Ordinary fitting follows. Internal fitting search has a separate
  classification and a stricter comparison arm.
- **Representation lineage:** CLOVER 2051, Candle 2048 and Hyperion 512 are distinct.
  A 512-dimensional `solution_embedding` is proposed, not retrofitted onto historical
  measurements. Closed complete-program support cannot establish out-of-catalog
  synthesis.
- **Objective:** native L6 supports/reductions are stated separately from a
  prospective first-proposal objective. A best-of-eight improvement is not evidence
  of first-proposal improvement. A finite-support KL anchor must not be reused
  unchanged to prohibit all novel-support actions.
- **Prospective experiment:** at least 240 planned independent confirmation families,
  subject to development-only power simulation, and separate mechanism confirmation.
  The proposed lower confidence-bound superiority threshold is 0.02 clipped utility
  units, not a 2% RMSE claim. This is not a completed preregistration.
- **Fair controls:** selection, retrieval, a distilled bank, conditional frontier
  generation, matched-support imitation, shuffled inputs and equal-compute histories.
  Universal authoring and per-task generation are distinct information/cost regimes.
- **Observed data:** a separately pinned four-artifact exploratory comparison,
  with training-family, preprocessing and adaptive-seed limitations beside the figure.
  No shared Elo axis combines this with a different engine ladder.
- **Novelty:** archive exclusion plus useful behavior and intervention, not a low
  probability of an exact source string. Move42 remains a prospective milestone.
- **Costs:** disjoint provider accounting; estimates remain estimates; cached input
  and reasoning are not added twice. Source expansion is not token retention.
  Both training and universal source authoring are amortized.
- **Disclosure:** public interfaces, outputs, supports, evidence and limitations;
  no new grammar production inventory or reversible proprietary representation.
- **Narrative:** Move37 and self-play motivate the question. Direct synthesis and
  symbolic-regression antecedents prevent a false priority claim. Socher, Recursive,
  Jev and LeCun are contextualized from primary sources, without unverified quotes.
- **Presentation:** Palatino text/math, restrained cyan/amber/ink, vector figures,
  source-backed numbers, readable code volume and shared bibliography. No simulated
  winning curves, illustrative performance numbers or placeholder empirical abstract.

## Corrections to historical material

The anonymous report C is repository-attributed to Opus; report D remains
unresolved and must not be assigned to the much larger Gemini replacement source.
Gemini's fractional seed-averaged match score differs from majority-family wins.
The Sol/Gemini geometric error ratio implies an 18.1% higher Gemini point estimate,
not 15%, and its reported interval includes parity. Different runtime hosts prohibit
a controlled speedup claim. Seven supplied benchmark payloads have one extra trailing
line feed relative to their pinned Git objects; both byte identities are retained in
the artifact manifest. Eighteen other payloads match exactly.

## Submission readiness

This PR supplies an auditable manuscript and build. It does not assert acceptance
by any venue or empirical readiness for a tier-1 results paper. Before submission,
confirm the author/contribution roster, choose the venue template and page budget,
complete dataset-rights review, freeze and register the prospective protocol, and
run the decisive experiments if an empirical claim is intended. A protocol or
position-paper submission should retain the current evidence-bounded framing.
