\section{The engine's programs}
\label{app:programs}

The ten programs of \cref{tab:programs} are committed under
\code{docs/paper/innovationzero/evidence/pilot-2026-10-22/programs/} and printed in full in the
companion archive, beside the four frontier entrants and the challenge they answered. \Cref{tab:manifest} lists them.
The excerpt below is from the smallest, the ridge program for \code{047e62b5}: its self-documenting
header and its fitting routine. Every emitted program begins with a plain-language description of
its pipeline, cites the origin of each method it uses, and solves its own linear algebra.

Two properties of this program are worth stating because a reader will find them. The ridge head
clips its predictions to $[-60, 60]$ in target units, a bound that is part of the emitted program and
would need revisiting for targets on a larger scale. And the command-line wrapper around \code{fit}
and \code{predict} reads and writes CSV files, which a challenge entrant may not do; the learning code
itself uses arithmetic, vectors and sorting only.
