\section{Claim ledger}
\label{app:claims}

Every quantitative statement in the paper, the file it comes from and its status. Paths beginning
\code{artifacts/}, \code{data/} or \code{evidence/} are relative to \code{docs/paper/innovationzero/};
the machine-readable ledger \code{evidence/claims.json} pins the repository-reported claims to full
revisions, paths and SHA-256 values. \est{} established:
committed to the repository and reproducible from it. \pre{} preliminary: an unmerged branch, a
non-qualifying host, a small panel, or an estimate. \pend{} pending. External facts are cited in the
text and listed in the references.
