the work among its precedents and the debate it enters. S2 audits the historical native objective at
the repository snapshot, term by term. S3 specifies the statistical and resource contract of the
proposed experiment. S4 reconciles the frontier authoring receipts phase by phase and states the
metering contract future sessions must follow. S5 summarizes the organizer's appendix to the
challenge, including the sixteen attacks closed before any entrant saw it. Proposed experiments
remain proposed. The companion archive reproduces the complete challenge, the four frontier sources
and the ten emitted programs byte for byte.
