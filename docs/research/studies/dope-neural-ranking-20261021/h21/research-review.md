# Research decision after the H20 null/adverse result

H20 completed all twelve wider-bound adapters and both primary raw-fitness points were adverse, with familywise intervals including harm. Another bound, checkpoint or seed sweep on the same exposed OOF panel is not the next priority. Resolve exact source-qualified volume first. Millions of candidate outcomes do not multiply the number of independent dataset components.

The accepted proof framework is semantic identity of typed rows, order, multiplicity, cut, columns/target and transformations, rather than identical CSV serialization. A proven identity or data-independent fixed transform can use a no-fit certificate instead of an inapplicable scaler-fit object. Neither rule permits inference from absent fields: the actual historical pipeline, selected rows, role, reservations and exact GP label/evaluator semantics still have to be established.

A later, more distinct model hypothesis is compatibility between real Dope state and the actual archived full-TRAIN fitted candidate's response on authorized TRAIN probes. It is deferred until source and decision-stage availability are qualified. A shared missing-response flag does not fix outcome leakage: winner-only or post-fitness artifact capture is unqualified. Require the original full attempt/slate capture policy, artifact creation stage and exact predictor/preprocessor/row identity; prohibit target-CV/OOF score reconstruction and all candidate/baseline refits. Charge acquisition, feature construction and inference costs. Existing state/DSL interaction and teacher/function proposals must be accounted for rather than relabeled as new.
