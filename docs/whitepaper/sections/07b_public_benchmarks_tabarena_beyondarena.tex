\subsection{Public benchmarks: TabArena v0.1 and BeyondArena 2026}
\label{sec:blackhole-public-benchmarks}

To evaluate how our three regimes behave outside \texttt{dev91} against both published community leaderboards and
identically executed local foundation baselines (\texttt{Kumo-Tabular} large and small, \texttt{TabFM 1.0} single, and
\texttt{TabPFN 3.5}) with zero external API calls, we pre-registered a two-wave protocol across \texttt{TabArena v0.1}
\citep{erickson2025tabarena} and \texttt{BeyondArena 2026} (\texttt{2ecfe882}) at official repeat~0, fold~0. Every
public task was screened by content digest and schema overlap (\texttt{da7e5780}) against our generalist training banks
(\texttt{r4}/\texttt{r5}), partitioning Wave~A ($44$ regression tasks: $36$ \texttt{BeyondArena} = $27$ unseen + $9$
in-corpus; $8$ \texttt{TabArena} = $1$ unseen + $7$ in-corpus) and Wave~B ($129$ classification tasks: $94$
\texttt{BeyondArena} = $32$ unseen + $62$ in-corpus; $35$ \texttt{TabArena} = $3$ unseen + $32$ in-corpus) into
single-regime slices while excluding sealed overlaps (\texttt{CONFIRM}/\texttt{HOLDOUT}).

\paragraph{Measured Wave~A Kumo calibration and two-phase barrier.}
In the pre-refresh published extract used at \texttt{LOOK\_LEDGER} line~48, neither Kumo model appeared; in the
2026-11-12 upstream \texttt{data.tabarena.ai} R2 refresh (\texttt{LOOK\_LEDGER} line~60; \texttt{verified=false} on
2026-10-06, \texttt{verified=true} at \texttt{tabarena@9f985c0f}), published \texttt{TA-KUMO-TABULAR} L/M/S rows cover
all $44$ tasks. Across all $44$ public
regression tasks ($88/88$ completed cells in \texttt{R\_K} \texttt{f63ff1ce}, zero failures), local \texttt{Kumo-Tabular}
large and small place $\#1$ ($1702.7$ Elo, $22$--$0$--$7$ vs.\ published \texttt{TabPFN 3.5} and $20$--$0$--$9$ vs.\
\texttt{TA-CAUSILO}) and $\#2$ ($1624.1$ Elo) of $31$ methods on \texttt{BeyondArena}'s $29$ full-TEST tasks ($5$--$0$--$2$
large vs.\ small on the $7$ subsampled $8{,}192$-row tasks), and $\#3$ ($2192.2$ Elo, $4$--$0$--$4$ vs.\ \texttt{TabPFN
3.5}; $2704.0$ $\#2$ minus the $3$ context-capped tasks with width-capped \texttt{QSAR-TID-11} kept) and $\#8$ ($1964.2$
Elo) of $71$ methods on \texttt{TabArena} (\cref{tab:public-waves}). Once \texttt{TabFM 1.0} single and our unabsorbed
system complete Phase~1, comparisons with our system are fitted strictly per regime before Phase~2 random-split dossier
absorption begins.
