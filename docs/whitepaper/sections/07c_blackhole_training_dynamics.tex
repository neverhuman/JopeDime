\label{sec:blackhole-dynamics}

To separate optimization progress on random-split training pools from out-of-pool generalization, we pair training
cross-entropy trajectories with two held-out probes that never guide checkpoint selection.

\paragraph{Dossier loss vs.\ fresh-row skill.}
Across all 91 per-table dossier runs (\cref{tab:blackhole-loss-curve}), median three-head cross-entropy falls from
4.515\,nats at 100 exposures per row to 1.984\,nats at 2,000 exposures per row at a median throughput of 7,367 query
rows/s per 24\,GB card. When 20\% of a table's rows are withheld completely outside the dossier's random-split pool,
cross-fitted utility rises from 0.852\,$u$ at initialization to 1.185\,$u$ at 500 exposures per row ($+0.333\,u$),
demonstrating that moderate random-split training learns transferable conditional structure on unseen rows of the same
domain; continuing from 500 to 2,000 exposures further lowers in-pool cross-entropy and drives the 2343.8--2408.5 Elo
absorbed benchmark scores while fresh-row utility settles at 1.043\,$u$ ($+0.191\,u$ above initialization).

\paragraph{32-table bank-disjoint generalist trajectory.}
Across our five-host generalist training fleet (4,826 real tables in 2,736 families, 129.9M cumulative dataset episodes
fed at 212--350 datasets/s, including 22.0M episodes under the random-split all-data policy), we evaluate milestone
$M$-scale (23.7M parameter) checkpoints on a pre-frozen 32-table validation panel (\texttt{30ccaa2a}) verified disjoint
from every historical training bank. Validation cross-entropy decreases monotonically across unseen-board checkpoints
($3.9569 \to 3.9282 \to 3.9087$\,nats; median NRMSE $0.8216 \to 0.8103$) and again across successive all-data banks
$\texttt{r3} \to \texttt{r4} \to \texttt{r5}$ ($3.9605 \to 3.9539 \to 3.9480$\,nats).

\paragraph{Kernel-embedding admission precision.}
Candidate feature maps are represented as continuous kernel embeddings $\encS(s)$ under a positive-definite similarity
kernel and gated by an inner cross-validation referee before entering the stack. Across 3,639 corpus tables, admitting
all candidates wins on only 56.2\% of held-out splits, positive-mean inner CV wins on 65.5\%, and requiring at least
$+0.5\,u$ gain over a fixed nonlinear dictionary + ridge baseline on \emph{every} inner fold achieves 99.3\% held-out
win precision (137 of 138 admitted tables, mean $+2.38\,u$ gain).
