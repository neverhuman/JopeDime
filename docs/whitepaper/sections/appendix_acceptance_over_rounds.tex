\begin{figure*}[!t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/fig_fw_latest_admissions.pdf}
  \caption{\textbf{Retained development utility through round 2837.}
  All \num{2063} admission records from rounds 775--2837 are shown: blue is the
  post-decision retained best; grey marks rejected candidates, red stars
  accepted strict gains and hollow diamonds accepted exact ties.
  The default rule accepts candidate utility $\geq$ the previous best,
  without a margin. Acceptance can therefore change the recorded
  best-round identity without raising utility. Ties are classified from
  unrounded logged values. These adaptive development observations require
  separate first-proposal and transfer evaluations.}
  \label{fig:fw-latest-admissions}
\end{figure*}

\Cref{fig:fw-acceptance-over-rounds} provides a companion view of the
\num{1889} consecutive admission rounds, 775--2663, plotted in
\cref{fig:fw-validation}. It adds no new observations. The left panel
isolates the post-decision recorded-best validation pool utility step
curve across the two strict improvements (rounds 785 and 859, reaching
1.1376 and 1.1378 from the initial 1.1074) and the two accepted ties
(rounds 782 and 882). The right panel tracks the cumulative share of
elapsed rounds accepted up to each round: the cumulative rate reaches
18.18\% at round 785 ($2/11$ rounds), stands at 3.70\% at the last
accepted tie ($4/108$ at round 882), and declines across the \num{1781}
subsequent rejected rounds to 0.21\% ($4/\num{1889}$) by round 2663.
