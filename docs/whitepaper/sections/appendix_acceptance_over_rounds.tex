\Cref{fig:fw-acceptance-over-rounds} combines the \num{1889} consecutive
admission evaluations across rounds 775--2663 (\cref{fig:fw-validation})
with their cumulative acceptance rate and candidate utility-shortfall
distribution. The left panel tracks candidate proposals against the
post-decision recorded-best validation pool utility across the two strict
improvements (rounds 785 and 859, reaching 1.1376 and 1.1378 from the
initial 1.1074) and the two accepted ties (rounds 782 and 882). The center
panel tracks the cumulative share of elapsed rounds accepted up to each
round: the cumulative rate reaches 18.18\% at round 785 ($2/11$ rounds),
stands at 3.70\% at the last accepted tie ($4/108$ at round 882), and
declines across the \num{1781} subsequent rejected rounds to 0.21\%
($4/\num{1889}$) by round 2663.
