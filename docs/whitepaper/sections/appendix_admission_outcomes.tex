\section{Recorded Admission Frequencies}\label{app:admission-outcomes}

\Cref{fig:fw-admission-outcomes} retains every completed decision in its
cumulative denominator. Admission includes exact ties; a separate line
counts only strict utility gains. Both are descriptive frequencies on
reused development contexts, under the limits in \cref{sec:method-status}.

\begin{figure}[htb]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/fig_fw_admission_outcomes.pdf}
  \caption{\textbf{Cumulative adapter admission frequencies.}
  All \num{2937} completed records in rounds 883--3819, captured at 18:27 UTC
  on 2 November 2026. At round $r$, the denominator is all $r-882$ decisions
  since round 883. Blue counts every admission; dashed red counts strict
  gains, and the shaded difference counts accepted ties. The final
  numerator is four: two strict gains and two ties; the other \num{2933}
  decisions are rejections. Classification uses unrounded logged values.
  These adaptive frequencies do not estimate independent proposal quality
  or a training effect. Scope: \cref{sec:method-status}.}
  \label{fig:fw-admission-outcomes}
\end{figure}
