\subsection{Event counts in retained development windows}\label{app:event-counts}

\Cref{fig:fw-event-distribution} summarizes five timed 20-round windows:
2343--2362, 2508--2527, 2644--2663, 2933--2952 and 3001--3020. It pairs
per-round event-count distributions with whole-window event rates and mean
round durations. The earlier untimed windows are not included in those rates.
\Cref{fig:fw-recent-development} separately shows trajectories for
2005--2024, 2082--2101, 2343--2362 and 2508--2527; the 2644--2663
trajectory is retained in \cref{fig:fw-program-promotions}.
The windows remain separate under the scope in \cref{sec:method-status}.
