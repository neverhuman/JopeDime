\subsection{Completed-round rates}\label{app:subsequent-rates}

For the 20 summaries in the fourth column of \cref{fig:fw-subsequent-rates},
rounds 2508--2527 total \num{1731.45}\,s and 15 strict program-incumbent
events. Dividing total counts by total logged time gives 41.58 completed
rounds per hour and 31.19 events per hour; these are not averages of per-round rates.
\Cref{fig:fw-latest-event-rates} places the event rate and mean round duration
alongside the other timed windows. The underlying per-round times remain
in the retained window record; they are not plotted again here.

Time includes logged qualification waits and omits gaps between
rounds. None records an adapter admission. Scope: \cref{sec:method-status}.
