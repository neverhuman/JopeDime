\subsection{Completed-round rates}\label{app:subsequent-rates}

The third and fourth panels of \cref{fig:fw-subsequent-rates} reuse the 20
summaries from rounds 2508--2527 in \cref{app:subsequent-window}. Each step
divides one completed round, or its strict training-program event count, by
that round's logged wall time. Its width is the corresponding duration.
Dashed lines divide the window's total work or events by its total time;
they do not average the per-round rates.

Time includes logged qualification waits and omits gaps outside completed
rounds. None records an adapter admission. Scope: \cref{sec:method-status}.
