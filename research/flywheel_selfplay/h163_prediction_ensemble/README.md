# H163 — Accepted adapter prediction ensemble

H163 tests whether prediction-level cost ensembling of the read-only accepted checkpoints R782/R785/R859/R882 improves the family-weighted out-of-fold utility of R882. The protocol, selector and the distinct directions of predicted cost (lower is preferred) and measured utility (higher is preferred) are frozen in [PREREG.md](PREREG.md).

Status: preregistration in progress; not executed. Zero optimizer updates. R882's historical exposure to the 1,219 TRAIN contexts limits the result to conditional continuation evidence. No promotion or serving change is authorized.
