Verify all non-adapter tensors bitwise unchanged before and after every update/evaluation. Keep the frozen H146/H156 forward/loss/cache implementation and fit minibatch recipe. No sealed, holdout, meta-test, synthetic, serving or live-flywheel access; no speed or AUC reporting.

Arms run 40 updates with global gradient clip L2=1 and snapshots 0/10/20/30/40: CTRL AdamW `(lr=1e-4, wd=1e-4, eps=1e-8)`; SGDM (momentum `.9`, wd `1e-4`); SGDM_NEST (same chosen lr, Nesterov); ADAM_EPS AdamW `(lr=1e-4, wd=1e-4, eps=1e-3)`. Before any selector/assessment outcome is opened, calibrate SGDM lr once from `{1e-3,3e-3,1e-2}` using only seed 169101, outer fold 0 FIT gradients: choose step-40 adapter L2 drift closest to CTRL step-40 drift (lower lr breaks ties). Calibration uses common FIT minibatches and records the durable choice/drifts. It does not use utility. Every absent `p.grad` is materialized as zero before clipping/optimizer step; this lets weight decay/momentum act on the six presently unconnected adapter tensors.

Three seeds `169101–169103`, family-grouped five-fold cross-fit on 1,219 TRAIN contexts / 205 families. Per outer fold, assess A, select B=(A+1)%5, fit the other three folds; all contexts are assessed exactly once per seed. Steps 0/10/20/30/40. Select earliest best equal-family selector mean (ties within `1e-12` go to earlier step); accept only gain `>0.001+1e-12` and family bootstrap 95% LCB>0, else step 0. Persist/fsync selection locks before any trained A or DL-Val evaluation. Also report inner-B unconstrained best-step, step-40 gain and adapter L2 drift descriptively.

Primary: equal-family OOF utility gain vs r882 per seed, median across three seeds, shared-family 10,000-draw bootstrap CI, one-sided p, Holm adjustment across SGDM/SGDM_NEST/ADAM_EPS; CTRL descriptive.
