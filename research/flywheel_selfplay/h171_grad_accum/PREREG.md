## Hypothesis and Non-Overlap

Across prior `r882` continuation waves (`H148`, `H154`, `H161`, `H162`, `H165`, `H166`, `H167`), strict inner-fold selectors overwhelmingly retain step `0` (`r882`) (`H156` used a point-positive `XFIT` primary and reported `NULL`; later diagnostics found `4/180` strict-eligible candidate snapshots (`1` `W10`, `3` `W05`), so it was not an all-zero strict-selection study, whereas `H161`, `H166`, and `H167` selected `0/15` strict non-zero cells), while single-minibatch (`|B| = 8` contexts) continuations exhibit a `~0.29` `DL-Train` vs `DL-Val` utility gap (`1.423` vs `1.138`) and degrade unconstrained out-of-fold utility by `-0.002` to `-0.030`. Because `DL-Train` spans `1,219` contexts across `205` families, an `8`-context minibatch samples `< 7%` of the `123` fit-split families per update, so each AdamW step chases high-variance, family-dominated gradients.

**Hypothesis (`H171`)**: Reducing stochastic gradient variance via **large effective batch size (`4B = 32` contexts across `32` distinct fit families in `ACC4`, and `16B = 128` contexts covering all `123/123` fit-split families per update in `ACC16`)** via exact mean-gradient accumulation stabilizes continuation directions (measurable as higher cosine similarity between each update gradient and the exact full-fit-split gradient $g_{\text{full}}(\theta)$) and separates *"steps are too noisy (`|B| = 8` chases a few families)"* from *"any step overfits the fit split."* To isolate *"more examples seen"* from *"less gradient noise per update"* at the `4B` dose, we include an example-matched live-batch control **`CTRL_EX4`** that runs `160` live single-minibatch (`|B| = 8`) AdamW updates on the **exact same `160` family-stratified minibatches** seen by `ACC4`, evaluated at example-matched snapshots `0, 40, 80, 120, 160`. (`ACC16` tests the full-fit-family `16B` accumulation dose directly without a `640`-update live-batch arm, so `ACC16` does not isolate accumulation from additional example exposure at `16B`; `ACC4` vs `CTRL_EX4` provides the clean example-matched mechanism isolation.)

No speed metric, AUC, binary classification proxy, or serving change is permitted.

## Family-Stratified Fit-Split Sampling & Registered Arms (`15` Seed-Fold Cells $\times$ `280` Updates/Cell = `4,200` Updates)

Within each outer fold $k \in \{0..4\}$ and seed $s \in \{171101, 171102, 171103\}$, let $\text{FIT}_k$ denote the `fit`-split context set (`123` families, disjoint from `select`, `assess`, and `DL-Val`).
- **Fit-Split Slates & Population Weights**: For each fit context $c \in \text{FIT}_k$, a deterministic `24`-candidate training slate (`4` top-fitness revealed candidates `order[:4]` plus `20` random unrevealed candidates sampled without replacement from `order[4:]` via `stream_seed(seed, outer, "fit_slate", c)`) is constructed solely from $\text{FIT}_k$. Each fit context $c \in C_f$ carries equal-family population weight $w_c = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{F}_{\text{fit}}| \cdot |C_f|}$ ($\sum_{c \in \text{FIT}_k} w_c = 1$, $|\mathcal{F}_{\text{fit}}| = 123$).
- **Family-Stratified Microbatch Plan**: For each macro-step $u \in \{1..40\}$, `family_stratified_plan` generates `16` microbatches $B_{u, 0}, B_{u, 1}, \dots, B_{u, 15}$ of `|B| = 8` contexts (`1` context sampled uniformly per family across `8` distinct fit families per microbatch) by concatenating random permutations of the `123` fit families (`stream_seed(seed, outer, "family_stratified_u", u)`), so that:
  - Every single `8`-context microbatch $B_{u, m}$ spans `8` distinct fit families,
  - The first `4` microbatches $B_{u, 0..3}$ (`32` contexts) span `32` distinct fit families, and
  - All `16` microbatches $B_{u, 0..15}$ (`128` contexts) cover all `123/123` fit families on every macro-step $u$.

### The Four Prespecified Arms
1. **`CTRL`** (`40` AdamW updates, `lr = 1e-4`, effective batch `1B = 8` contexts / `8` families):
   - At update $u \in \{1..40\}$, computes gradient on microbatch $B_{u, 0}$, clips at `L2 = 1.0`, and steps `AdamW`. Snapshots saved at updates `0, 10, 20, 30, 40`.
2. **`ACC4`** (`40` AdamW updates, `lr = 1e-4`, effective batch `4B = 32` contexts / `32` distinct families):
   - At update $u \in \{1..40\}$, accumulates the exact mean gradient $\frac{1}{4}\sum_{m=0}^{3} \nabla_\theta \ell(B_{u, m})$ across microbatches $B_{u, 0..3}$, clips at `L2 = 1.0`, and steps `AdamW`. Snapshots saved at updates `0, 10, 20, 30, 40`.
3. **`ACC16`** (`40` AdamW updates, `lr = 1e-4`, effective batch `16B = 128` contexts covering all `123` fit families):
   - At update $u \in \{1..40\}$, accumulates the exact mean gradient $\frac{1}{16}\sum_{m=0}^{15} \nabla_\theta \ell(B_{u, m})$ across microbatches $B_{u, 0..15}$, clips at `L2 = 1.0`, and steps `AdamW`. Snapshots saved at updates `0, 10, 20, 30, 40`.
4. **`CTRL_EX4`** (`160` AdamW updates, `lr = 1e-4`, effective batch `1B = 8` contexts, example-matched to `ACC4`):
   - Steps `AdamW` once on each of the `160` microbatches $B_{1, 0..3}, B_{2, 0..3}, \dots, B_{40, 0..3}$ in the exact same order seen by `ACC4`. Snapshots saved at **example-matched updates `0, 40, 80, 120, 160`** (mapped to logical checkpoint indices `0, 10, 20, 30, 40`, having seen `0, 320, 640, 960, 1,280` context examples — identical to `ACC4` at updates `0, 10, 20, 30, 40`).

### Gradient-Noise Diagnostic (`cosine of each update grad to the full-fit-split grad`)
- Let $g_{\text{full}}(\theta) = \nabla_\theta \sum_{c \in \text{FIT}_k} w_c \, \ell(\theta; c) \in \mathbb{R}^{29211}$ (`None` $\to 0$ on the `6` no-grad tensors) denote the exact equal-family-weighted full-fit-split gradient over all `|\text{FIT}_k|` fit contexts (`123` families).
- **Exact Instantaneous Full-Fit Alignment (`CTRL`, `ACC4`, `ACC16` `100%` Instantaneous; `CTRL_EX4` `41` Macro-Step Checkpoints)**:
  - For **`CTRL`, `ACC4`, and `ACC16`** (`40` updates per trajectory), $g_{\text{full}}(\theta_{u-1})$ is recomputed at the **exact instantaneous pre-update parameter state $\theta_{u-1}$ on every single update $u \in \{1..40\}$** (`40/40 = 100%` instantaneous full-fit-split gradient evaluations per trajectory, `600` per arm across the `15` cells; `lagged_offgrid_evals = 0`).
  - For **`CTRL_EX4`** (`160` single-minibatch updates = `40` macro-groups of `4` microbatches), $g_{\text{full}}(\theta)$ is recomputed at the **exact instantaneous pre-update weights at the start of every macro-group $t = 4(u-1) + 1 \in \{1, 5, 9, \dots, 157\}$ plus the final update $t = 160$** (`41` exact instantaneous evaluations per trajectory, `615` across the `15` cells). Off-grid intermediate sub-updates $m \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ within each `4`-step macro-group record `exact_instantaneous_full_fit = false`, `reference_full_fit_update = 4(u-1) + 1`, `reference_full_fit_lag_updates = m`, `cos_to_full_fit_grad = null`, and `cos_to_lagged_macrostep_full_fit_grad`.
  - Noise alignment in `gradient_noise_diagnostic` is reported using **exact instantaneous rows only** (`exact_instantaneous_cos_*`), never mixing lagged off-grid rows into instantaneous averages. Every update across all `4` arms also logs `cos_to_r882_full_fit_grad`.

- **Primary Estimand**: Equal-family mean out-of-fold utility gain vs `r882` on `1,219`-context / `205`-family `DL-Train` for each seed, aggregated by the **median of the three scalar seed gains** (`10,000` shared family-bootstrap draws; percentile 95% CI; one-sided bootstrap $p$-value with Holm step-down across the `3` treatment arms `ACC4`, `ACC16`, `CTRL_EX4` vs `r882`, $m = 3$).
