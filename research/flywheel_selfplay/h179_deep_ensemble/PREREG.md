# H179 — independent fresh-adapter deep ensemble versus r882

Owner: `luna_ns_hbaseline`; assignment `~/nudges/hwave_t0510_h179_hbaseline.md`. Diagnostic study only. It cannot promote a model, change the live flywheel, or authorize serving changes.

## Question and locked design

Does averaging predictions from independently initialized fresh adapters improve held-out A-family utility over the zero-update r882 reference, compared with one fresh adapter and 40-step r882 continuation?

Use the locally restored, provenance-verified real-S3 TRAIN closure of 1,219 contexts / 205 families and the exact cached frozen-DOPE feature matrix. Split families using seed 179140 into five groups of 41. For outer fold `j`, A is group `j`, B is group `(j+1) mod 5`, and FIT is the remaining 123 families. B is disclosed as unused and never supplies training, selection, or evaluation. Evaluate every selected family once across the five folds, pooled to all 205 A families for each outer seed. If the fixed timing pilot projects the full design beyond three hours, use A groups 0, 1, and 2 only and disclose the resulting 123-family coverage.

The arms per outer-seed/fold are R882 (zero-update native reference), CONT (private r882 initialization with the live H176 continuation recipe and 40 updates), FRESH1 (member 1), ENS3 (mean score of members 1–3), and ENS5 (mean score of members 1–5). Five fresh members train independently on the same 123 FIT families, each with the H176 fresh-adapter recipe and exactly 40 updates. Seeds are outer `179101, 179102, 179103`; the corresponding member seeds are `179201–179205`, `179301–179305`, and `179401–179405`. Member initialization and batch streams are independent; ENS3 is nested within ENS5. All arms use identical optimizer settings and update counts. Only the 29,211 trainable adapter parameters change on private in-memory model copies. Frozen DOPE and all non-adapter state are checked throughout.

For each candidate in each A slate, average members' raw `total_cost` scores (the selector's score space) before `argmin`. Do not average choices or weights. The one-member FRESH1 score is member 1's score. No A outcomes are used for gradient updates, member/checkpoint selection, or timing choices. The training batches contain eight FIT contexts and the fixed H156 candidate recipe (four lowest raw-fitness candidates plus twenty sampled without replacement), with the H176 loss and AdamW settings. CONT and every fresh member receive 40 updates unless the timing-only rule selects a smaller common count for all arms.

## Outcomes and decision

Report for each arm median equal-family utility across the three outer seeds; paired family-bootstrap gain versus R882 and percentile 95% CI (10,000 shared family resamples); Bradley–Terry Elo against R882=1500 and paired family-bootstrap 95% CI; each seed's gain; exact count of `-2.0` selections; and the pairwise choice-disagreement rate among all five fresh members on A contexts. Average candidate scores before every ensemble argmin. Report no AUC or speed metrics.

The three fresh-arm comparisons FRESH1, ENS3, ENS5 use one-sided centered paired-family bootstrap tests; adjust all three p-values with Holm step-down (m=3). `ADVANCE_CANDIDATE` requires all of: ENS5 median OOF gain strictly greater than `+0.001`, paired family-bootstrap LCB95 strictly greater than zero, all three seed gains strictly positive, and Holm-adjusted ENS5 p-value strictly below 0.05. Ties reject. Otherwise verdict is `NO_ADVANCE`. For a NO_ADVANCE result, label it an underpowered null only when the absolute ENS5 median gain is below the H175 full-slate family-clustered MDE80 planning heuristic `0.042341` (measured H175 MDE80 0.094678 divided by sqrt(5)); explicitly state this is a planning heuristic, not measured H179 power. An `ADVANCE_CANDIDATE` is a research handoff to the flywheel owner and is not adopted by this study.
