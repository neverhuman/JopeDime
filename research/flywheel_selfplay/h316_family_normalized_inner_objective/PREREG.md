## NORM objective and ambiguity resolution
The instruction explicitly says **per inner-TRAIN context**. Family-grouped inner validation families are absent from inner-TRAIN, so their within-family TRAIN MAD would be undefined. Therefore NORM evaluates the frozen inner member predictions on that inner fold's TRAIN contexts, using only those contexts' utilities. This is an in-sample inner selection objective, reported honestly; BASE retains unchanged H303 inner out-of-fold selection. No fallback to validation-family scales and no outer information.
For each inner fold and config, for each inner-TRAIN family f, scale_f = max(median(abs(u_slot0 - median(u_slot0))) over that family's inner-TRAIN contexts, 0.001). This is unscaled MAD (no 1.4826 multiplier). gain_c = (u_pick - u_slot0)/scale_f. Compute pooled-context q10/q90 within that fold and config using numpy linear quantiles; winsorize to those endpoints. Across four inner folds, aggregate the repeated training-context gains by family (each family appears in three inner-TRAIN folds); family score = arithmetic mean of its clipped context records. Objective = median of the 205 outer-TRAIN family scores. Families absent from outer-TRAIN are never used. Ties within 1e-4 of the best go to fewer switched inner-TRAIN prediction records, then H303 order (larger k, larger a, smaller tau). Do not tune this interpretation after seeing results.
Refit on outer-TRAIN only for NORM's selected tau (reuse exact BASE refit if tau matches). Persist chosen configs, inner tables, models and zero pre-lock outer access receipt in fsynced fit_lock; fsync parent directory. Only then read/score the outer fold once for NORM. BASE outer picks are reused, never rescored.

## Metrics and decision
On raw unnormalized outer utility report each partition/seed: family-equal mean gain and median across families, drop-top-1/5/10 family-equal mean gains, family W/T/L (epsilon 1e-9), harmed share (family loss >0.01), invalids, context switch rate and H302 Elo. Elo label is **reference = r882 slot0 (DL-Train outer)**; r3175 is not measured on TRAIN. The published H303/H308 median means median across training seeds of the family-equal mean; retain this statistic for exact regression and the 90% retention gate, while also reporting literal family medians separately.
NORM_ADVANCE iff median across all nine partition/root cells of (NORM drop-top-10 - BASE drop-top-10) >0 AND its paired whole-family bootstrap one-sided LCB95 >0 (10,000 resamples, numpy RNG 316160; same family indices for both arms/all cells; each resample re-drops each arm's own top ten); AND NORM median family-equal mean gain across nine cells >=90% of BASE; AND NORM median harmed share <=BASE. Two-sided 95% CI = quantiles .025/.975; LCB95 = .05. Ties never advance. Else NORM_NOT_ADVANCED. If advanced, propose a separate one-shot dev91 vs r3175, never run it here. Report H315 MARGIN pick overlap only if its closeout is already published; never wait.
