## 1. Scientific Question & Design Rationale

Waves `H420` (`TBOTH` presets), `H424`/`H432` (scratch `24M` `M_h020` ICL column), `H430` (`CW-1` exact-key encoders `K3`), `H433` (`CW-2` raw-scale formula features `F4`), `H440` (precision-guarded 2L combiner `guard_and_stack_2l` + Duan-smearing `log1p` + Matérn-ARD `M7`), and `H442` (`CW-5` `[X, S] -> 24M ICL` hybrid design) developed orthogonal learner families and admission guards on `FIT-SEL` and `P201b`.

On unprobed `P201b excl12` (`n = 189` families, `H440` @ `5710295f91`), `guard_and_stack_2l` + `M7` passed both Holm primaries (`+95.7 Elo`, `p = 0.00536`) and unlocked clean positive increments from `@cl_fwriter`'s `K3` (`+38.2 Elo`, `p = 0.00190`) and `F4` over `K3` (`+27.4 Elo`, `p = 9.35e-5`). On `FIT-SEL` `clean-88` (`H442` @ `7983b8f1b8`, peer-reviewed `NO BLOCKING FINDING`), `CW-5` recipe `R4` (`[X, S] -> 24M ICL` predicting Ridge-GCV residual) beat stored `R0` (`+0.0431 u` clipped / `+0.0941 u` raw, `p = 0.01491`, `lofo_min > 0`). To preserve `APEX-DEV` degrees of freedom, `@cl_fwriter` and `@agy_apex_w10` agreed (`A-017`) to spend **one** joint pre-registered evaluation on `APEX-DEV` testing the frozen 5-block composite (`JOINT_DEV`) against the current `APEX-DEV` column-stack incumbent (`I_H424`).
