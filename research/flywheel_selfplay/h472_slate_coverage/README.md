## Next step
H457–H472 have now varied the optimizer path (averaging, ASAM, dose, schedules, noise, Lookahead, accumulation), the loss shape (ctxnorm, tail clip, pair weight, robust/trimmed, label smoothing) and the slate composition, and nothing moves the ~57% non-floor win rate of kept changes. Arms either keep a subset of the control's changes (the dropped ones are net positive) or add extras at a coin-flip ~50% win rate (sl384). The pairwise Bradley-Terry term against the incumbent's pick weighs every better/worse pair equally, however small the fitness gap, and 25% of passing pairs sit within 0.25 of the context's passing-fitness spread. **H473** makes that term cost-sensitive at the control's compute and on its random streams: `pgap_z40` (pair weight ∝ |Δfitness| / spread, renormalized to mean 1 per context) and `pm25_z40` (strict-improvement dead zone that drops pairs with |Δfitness| ≤ 0.25 spread), against the `pairnf_z40` control. The prediction is that if near-tie pairs teach the coin-flip changes, the kept changes' non-floor win rate rises above ~57% and the paired non-floor gain CI clears 0.

## Hypothesis (pre-registered in #2391)
H457–H471 were all `NO_LIFT`. Every one of them perturbed the **optimizer path** of the 16-step gated pairnf step: Polyak/ASAM (H457), step dose (H461), cosine (H463), objective shape (H466), accumulation/Lookahead/label smoothing (H467–H468), robust losses (H469), gradient noise (H470) and lr-schedule shape (H471). Each arm either reproduced the control's kept changes or kept a **subset** of them, because its z-margins were smaller, and the dropped changes were net positive. The validation plateau (val91 utility ≈ 1.134–1.139, train/val gap ≈ 0.29, ~91–94% ties because the z ≥ 0.40 gate reverts to the incumbent pick) has not moved.

H472 changes the **training signal's coverage** instead. Every pool holds 1,151–1,152 programs. Each training slate holds 192 of them: the 48 best by true fitness, the incumbent's own pick and ~143 random programs. That is 16.7% of the pool per context per step. But the gate acts on an argmin over the **whole** pool, so most programs the adapter can switch to were rarely or never in a slate. Slate composition has never been varied on this instrument. H454 `hn16` forced the incumbent's predicted top-16 into the slate, which tests hardness, not coverage (it kept a subset and its CI spanned 0). The question is whether wider coverage of the pool (more random programs) makes the pairwise and listwise signal around the incumbent's pick generalize to held-out families, or whether more head programs (sharper near the optimum) do. Every arm shares the control's random streams (`rng_arm = pairnf`: init perturbation, context draws, dropout). The slate's random fill comes from the same keyed generator, so only the slate composition differs. The runs use 16 steps, lr 3e-4 and the unchanged z ≥ 0.40 gate:
- `pairnf_z40`: the control (H452–H471 pairnf + z ≥ 0.40, slate 192, keep 48). Adapter shas must equal the H471 `pairnf_z40` cells.
- `sl384_z40`: **coverage**, slate 384, keep 48. The same 48-program head plus the incumbent pick and ~335 random programs (33% of the pool per step). This doubles the forward/backward cost per step. It is not compute-matched by design, since the question is coverage.
- `kp96_z40`: **head focus**, slate 192, keep 96. The 96 best by fitness plus the incumbent pick and ~95 random programs, at the control's compute.

The two arms move the random/head split in opposite directions around the control, so a monotone result (sl384 > control > kp96 or the reverse) is interpretable. A U-shape or flat result says slate composition is not the bottleneck.

Not repeated (already falsified on this instrument): Polyak/EMA tail and ASAM (H457), 32-step dose and fbal (H461), recalibrated gate and 32-step cosine (H463), ctxnorm, tail clip and pair weight 2 (H466), acc2, Lookahead and BT label smoothing (H467), acc4 and displacement-matched Lookahead (H468), trim10/trim25/Cauchy (H469), gradient noise (H470), one-cycle/SGDR schedules (H471), hard-negative top-16 slate (H454), ensembles/soups/agreement gates (H453). Weight decay and modality dropout are inert at this horizon (H469/H471 design checks), so they are not tested.

## Pre-registered rules (unchanged from H452–H471)
- **Primary metric:** median across 3 seeds of the pooled OOF Elo vs the incumbent, with 95% family-bootstrap CIs (families resampled with all their rows in both partition copies). **Non-floor Elo** is co-primary and drives selection. Elo only, no AUC.
- **Selection:** per seed, the max mean non-floor OOF Elo over the two partitions (ties broken by pooled gain).
- **Strict:** pooled OOF gain > 0.002 and gain CI low > 0; pooled Elo > 0 and Elo CI low > 0; non-floor Elo CI low > 0; Elo > 0 on each partition. Ties never win, and a tie with the control is not accepted.
- An arm counts only if it also beats the control in the paired family bootstrap (arm − control CI low > 0).
- **LIFT** requires a non-control arm that passes strict and beats the control on every seed, val91 not_worse on every seed, a clean red-team **and** a median val91 gain beyond its cross-seed family-bootstrap CI (CI low > 0). Otherwise the verdict is `NO_LIFT`.

## Pre-registered red-team checks
- **Floor-recovery confound:** pooled gain from recovering −2.0 gate-fail contexts is not lift. Report non-floor gain/Elo, `recovered_gatefails` and `new_gatefails` per arm and seed.
- **Displacement / z-inflation confound:** report kept counts, the median |z| of changed picks vs the control and the kept-set overlap (subset / superset / extras) with the non-floor W/L of the extras and of the dropped control changes. An arm that keeps < 0.5× the control's changes is flagged gate-starved, and > 2× z-inflated (cf. H461 s32).
- **Compute confound (sl384):** sl384 does 2× the per-step work. If it wins, the closeout must check whether the win comes from new extras (coverage) or from z-inflation, using the kept-overlap and |z| report above.
- **Control reuse:** the 30 control CV cells per seed are **copied** from H471 `results/fold_sig` (sha256 list in `control_fold_sig_from_h471.sha256`), because the control was bit-identical across H466–H471 (30/30 each wave). The harness resumes them instead of retraining. The control is re-trained fresh in the smoke (cell h452a fold 0 seed 1, which must equal sha `8d5ac793…` and the OOF vector) and in the probe (full-train sha must equal `5f3acc50…`). The full-train control build for val91 (if selected) is re-trained fresh.
- **Tie acceptance:** ties never win. Report W/T/L everywhere, and treat a val91 gain ≈ 0 with ≥ 85/91 ties as not evidence.
- **Equal-Elo coincidences:** equal pooled Elos with equal W − L are reported as arithmetic coincidences after checking that the adapters and decisions differ.
- **No label leakage:** slate composition uses training-fold fitness only (as the control does). Held-out folds are scored by the label-free z-gate, and val91 is read once per seed for the selected arm only.
