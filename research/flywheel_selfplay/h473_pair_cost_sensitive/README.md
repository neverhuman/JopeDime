H473 found the first lever that changes the **floor** side consistently: gap weighting recovers 2–5 more incumbent gate failures per seed, with the same extra contexts on every seed and 0 new gate failures. ## Hypothesis (pre-registered in #2404)
H457–H472 were all `NO_LIFT`. Optimizer-path arms (Polyak/ASAM H457, dose H461, cosine H463, objective shape H466, accumulation/Lookahead/label smoothing H467–H468, robust losses H469, gradient noise H470, lr-schedule shape H471) keep a **subset** of the control's changes, because their z-margins are smaller, and the dropped changes are net positive. H472 changed slate composition. Coverage (`sl384`) added 83–98 extra kept changes per seed at a **coin-flip** non-floor win rate (W/L 42/41, 40/49, 51/47), against the control's ~57%. Head focus (`kp96`) dropped net-positive changes. In every wave, 52–66% of the control's pooled OOF gain is −2.0 floor recoveries, and every arm's non-floor gain CI spans 0. The validation plateau (val91 utility ≈ 1.134–1.139, ~91–94% ties at the z ≥ 0.40 gate) has not moved.

What none of them changed is **which pairwise comparisons the adapter is taught**. The pairnf Bradley-Terry term compares every passing slate program with the incumbent's own pick and weighs each better/worse pair **equally**, however small the true fitness gap. Over the packet's pools, 25% of passing pairs sit within 0.25 of the context's passing-fitness spread (std), 13% within 0.10 and 7% within 0.05 (the median is 0.83). Those near-tie pairs carry almost no utility, but they get the same gradient as large-gap pairs. So they can teach rank flips that the gate keeps and that come out as coin flips on held-out families. H473 makes the pairwise term **cost-sensitive** and leaves everything else at the control. That means 16 steps, lr 3e-4, slate 192, keep 48, the same pointwise/listwise/hinge terms, the z ≥ 0.40 gate, and every random stream (`rng_arm = pairnf`: init perturbation, context draws, slate fill, dropout):
- `pairnf_z40`: the control (H452–H472 pairnf + z ≥ 0.40). Adapter shas must equal the H472/H471 `pairnf_z40` cells.
- `pgap_z40`: **gap-weighted pairs** (LambdaRank-style). Each active pair gets weight |t_j − t_inc| / spread_c, where spread_c is the context's passing-fitness std from training labels. Weights are renormalized to mean 1 over the context's active pairs, so the pairwise loss scale, and to first order the adapter displacement, match the control.
- `pm25_z40`: **strict-improvement dead zone**. Pairs with |t_j − t_inc| ≤ 0.25 · spread_c are dropped (about a quarter of passing pairs), and the rest keep weight 1. The per-context mean over the remaining pairs keeps the loss scale.

The two arms differ in how they suppress near-tie pairs: a soft weight in `pgap`, a hard cut in `pm25`. If near-tie pairs drive the coin-flip changes, both should raise the non-floor win rate of kept changes above the control's ~57% and give a paired non-floor gain CI > 0. If both match the control, the pairwise label granularity is not the bottleneck.

Not repeated (already falsified on this instrument): Polyak/EMA tail and ASAM (H457), 32-step dose and fbal (H461), recalibrated gate and 32-step cosine (H463), ctxnorm, tail clip and pair weight 2 (H466), acc2, Lookahead and BT label smoothing (H467), acc4 and displacement-matched Lookahead (H468), trim10/trim25/Cauchy (H469), gradient noise (H470), one-cycle/SGDR schedules (H471), slate coverage/head focus (H472), hard-negative top-16 slate and z-margin pair focus (H454), ensembles/soups/agreement gates (H453), regret curricula (H446–H450). Gradient clipping is already on in every arm (global norm 1.0). Weight decay and modality dropout are inert at this horizon (H469/H471 design checks).

## Pre-registered rules (unchanged from H452–H472)
- **Primary metric:** median across 3 seeds of the pooled OOF Elo vs the incumbent, with 95% family-bootstrap CIs (families resampled with all their rows in both partition copies). **Non-floor Elo** is co-primary and drives selection. Elo only, no AUC.
- **Selection:** per seed, the max mean non-floor OOF Elo over the two partitions (ties broken by pooled gain).
- **Strict:** pooled OOF gain > 0.002 and gain CI low > 0; pooled Elo > 0 and Elo CI low > 0; non-floor Elo CI low > 0; Elo > 0 on each partition. Ties never win, and a tie with the control is not accepted.
- An arm counts only if it also beats the control in the paired family bootstrap (arm − control CI low > 0).
- **LIFT** requires a non-control arm that passes strict and beats the control on every seed, val91 not_worse on every seed, a clean red-team **and** a median val91 gain beyond its cross-seed family-bootstrap CI (CI low > 0). Otherwise the verdict is `NO_LIFT`.
