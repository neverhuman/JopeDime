### W9 — Causal flywheel closeout

- **Hypothesis:** updated guidance acquires better data, and those data causally improve the next guidance model at equal resource cost.
- **Arms:** cross frozen/updated ranker with frozen/fresh memory, giving four cells. Every cell executes the same acquisition budget; frozen-memory cells retain outcomes in a shadow log without feeding them back. Add the strongest W1 non-neural policy, including the context-aware tree if it wins, as a comparator. Then replay each cell's produced memory into the **same** initialization/training recipe, once with the neural learner and once with the strongest W1 tree learner. Match row counts, status mix, fit budget and optimizer updates.
- **Protocol:** seeds 701–703, five family folds, two fixed acquisition/learning cycles; freeze all settings from the selected development recipe before the closeout starts. ≤9,600 total native calls and 4 GPU-hours, four CPU-hours; scale the predeclared context-round roster down prospectively if the timing pilot cannot fit. No live deployment, no new DL-Val scoring and no chosen peak checkpoint.
- **Metrics / success / kill:** raw family utility, Elo, U4 and actual optimizer/parameter changes. Require fresh+updated to beat both one-sided controls and the strongest non-neural matched baseline, and require its memory to improve the common learner over uniform-acquisition memory. Report the 2×2 interaction, both cycle increments and all costs. A positive interaction alone is insufficient; a higher pool oracle without learner improvement is not a neural flywheel. If the two directions of benefit are not both supported, retain the narrower search or supervised-learning result and reject the breakthrough claim.
- **Data / budget:** only prospective admitted training outcomes under the common closure; legacy S3 imports, if used, are identical initial memory in every cell. Full receipt provenance retained, no additional prefix access. 4 GPU-hours, 9,600 native calls maximum.
- **Support:** I-A6, OPUS strongest-baseline requirement, ARGON data-generation ideas; this is the required closeout, not an optional celebratory benchmark.

## Deferred or rejected directions

- **Sibling-label consensus:** rejected under the current target-row closure. Family membership and `nq=0` alone do not authorize it.
- **Standalone persistent training, EMA, more steps or another shallow ES sweep:** overlaps H48/H50/H51/H55/H57. Changed-contract factorials may follow W1–W3, with matched total updates and fixed endpoints.
- **Pure acceptance-rule relaxation:** can admit more checkpoints without improving capability. Serving and training must eventually be separated, but neither fold rotation nor deterministic rescoring supplies a fresh statistical test.
- **Generation/birth index as a deployed predictor:** useful diagnostic of teacher selection, potentially unavailable or uninformative for novel same-generation proposals. Do not turn retrospective outcome-selected metadata into a free inference feature.
- **DOPE unfreezing, new holdouts, a broader S3 crawl or live-loop changes:** outside this proposal; any such change requires a separate owner decision.
