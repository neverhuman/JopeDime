H02 geometry and H06 matched neural controls did not establish incremental state value. Later H09's DSL-preserving residual was adverse in point estimate with an interval crossing zero.

H10–H16 were largely data-source/admission work: metadata or stored-context PASS did not supply original rows, scaler/cut authority or valid new ranking labels. H17's favorable nonstate-NN comparison was exposed, posthoc, incomplete common support with different features/training; it did not establish a DOPE effect. H18/H20 and DL03–DL07 retained actual updates and negative/uncertain primary outcomes; training-loss decline did not establish selection improvement. H27 completed 34,000 probe fits and 36 neural models, but both registered landmark contrasts included harm. H28 showed rare gate-failure transitions drove large differences. H30/H31/H33/H34 did not establish the proposed joint criteria; H34 GAP beat ORDINAL but not matched MSE. The archived W1 measured-history result was favorable on exposed fixed support, with independently reproduced replay, not new-family or live-flywheel proof.

H35–H40 reused cached outcomes and showed small apparent loop gains, but H41–H45 failed KL/non-superiority qualification: guided arms did not beat UNGUIDED, and cluster generations retained the incumbent. These curves cannot support a qualified self-improving production-loop claim.

H46–H100 repeatedly tested optimizers, objectives, evolution, averaging, losses and sampling against r785/r882. Strict wins were absent or incomplete. H56 exposed mismatch between displayed round histories and actual run/data/protocol lineage. H61's 0/240 candidate admissions showed that changing the admission rule alone could not rescue that candidate stream. H62 found slate/context tolerance issues, H63/H95 showed split-cut sensitivity, and H58's progressive fixes improved rank correlation while worsening top-1 utility. Surrogate fit and ranking correlation are therefore secondary, not substitutes for raw family utility.

The W-series continuation is also informative: H92's downside-controlled cached transfer passed a development screen, but H94 prospective continuation did not advance. H101's generic-prior contrast passed while the registered stronger retrieval/unseen-structure/placebo criteria failed. H108 established no neural residual gain over strong GBM/step-zero controls. H116/H120 closed adaptive 2+2 reveal at the tested four-of-sixteen budget. H118 showed generic scorer transfer to outside-roster families, but not the full registered W6 advancement. W5 H98/H102/H117/H150 retained unresolved parent-child/original-row provenance, so more provenance-only relabeling does not create training signal.

H125–H174 show a broad pattern: continuation often harms utility; regularizers, Lookahead, SWA/EMA, smoothing, Mixup, noise, optimizer changes and strict inner selection either failed or selected step zero. H139 identified selection artifacts. H151 found Lookahead's benefit explained by step-size/drift damping. H156/H168 did not establish wider-adapter gains. H172/H174 are incomplete, not numerical nulls. Repeating similar optimizer sweeps on the same 205 families has low expected value.

## Strongest recent mechanisms and limits

- H175 found substantial oracle headroom and family-sparsity/power limits. It did not identify a trainable oracle. H178's exact pooled H177 selector achieved `CALIBRATED_PASS_POWER_OK`: zero admissions in each retained null/harmful cell; family MDE80 was +.003038/+ .005057 under independent seed masks and +.016364/+ .024480 under cloned-seed dependence, for 24/full slates. Preserve the calibration scope and correlated-family caveat.
- H176's `DATA_SATURATED` applies only to fixed adapter/objective nested subsets of the existing 205-family corpus. It does not show that thousands of fresh S3 regression families are useless.
- H180's preregistered verdict is `ADAPTER_MATTERS` plus `BUDGET_FLAT`. Removing the adapter loses .0563 utility, CI [−.0977,−.0144]. A random adapter restores this structural benefit; training 40/160 updates adds no detectable gain under this recipe. The posthoc RAND0−ZERO contrast is +.0599, CI [+.0355,+.0917]. This is not a formal random/trained equivalence proof.
- H184 decomposes that structural benefit: `CARRIERS:KERNEL`, kernel contribution +.0613, CI [+.0296,+.0890], Holm p=.0003; prior and attention terms were neutral in this test. Revealed-history conditioning is real useful structure; trained-weight improvement remains unestablished.

- H196 attempt 4 is incomplete; its batch-shape comparator mismatch is infrastructure/numerical identity evidence, not efficacy. H207 must preserve exact singleton identity and re-register rather than loosen tolerance or reuse partial results.
- H198's anchor-only test-time training is inert at its tested k/lr range. H202 hard-negative point +.01089685 has CI [−.00461796,+.02568691] and 25.37% family harm: no advancement.

## Direction for the already owned new waves

Existing H204/H205/H206 plans address the two real bottlenecks: fresh source-qualified family diversity and representation/proposer learning rather than another frozen-adapter optimizer variant. Reuse their owners and IDs; do not launch duplicates. H205 is the priority causal test: equal-compute FRESH, STATIC-DATA and STATIC-TEACHER arms from r882, with fresh regression families and verified Rust-CV labels, measured updates/parameter deltas, pooled family selector and protected confirmation. H204's capacity/unfrozen-DOPE question requires a compute-matched continuation and random-adapter control, so any benefit can be attributed to capacity/representation rather than mere GPU time. H206 should produce the best-of-k search curve and then test k=1 distilled proposer gain at matched total labeling/training cost.

Every wave must report model generation versus measured family utility and Bradley–Terry ELO with a fixed common contender panel, family W/T/L, bootstrap uncertainty and harms. Keep candidate-generation, acquisition and common-learner memory benefits separate until all registered causal directions pass. GPU residency or a higher round number is not a learning receipt.

S3 use should maximize qualified coverage, not bytes downloaded. Use versioned content-addressed datasets, verified original TRAIN cuts, transitive family/duplicate closure and untouched confirmation roles. The current JRB admission follow-up must close its real-catalog key-format/transitive-family and binary-source-file scope bugs before fresh families are used. Stream/check bounded per-host caches; persist labels once and reuse only under identical task/dataset/cut/evaluator/program/protocol identities. Track newly admitted independent families separately from folds, repeats and candidate rows. Use original labels without artificial clipping/relabeling except explicitly preregistered loss transformations.
